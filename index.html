About us

We are a group of students who like making things people actually use. No experience needed. If you can be curious for an hour a week, you can join.

Learn

Weekly workshops on Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase and app design, taught by seniors and guest mentors.

Build

Join a project team and work like a real product studio: issues, code reviews and demos.

Ship

We help you publish to the Play Store and take apps to hackathons and competitions.
